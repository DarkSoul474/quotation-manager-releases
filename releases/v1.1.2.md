# Quotation Manager v1.1.2

## Bill and Proforma closing-summary layout refinement

v1.1.2 is a focused maintenance revision built on the accepted v1.1.1 production baseline. It changes only the presentation order of the Bill/Tax Invoice and Proforma Invoice closing summaries so the payable amount is shown before the rate-wise GST breakdown.

### Change

- In Bill/Tax Invoice and Proforma Invoice PDF, Word and Excel exports, the amount summary now appears first on the right side in this order: Sub Total Amount, Total CGST, Total SGST, Total IGST, Round Off and NET AMOUNT.
- The rate-wise GST summary now appears immediately underneath the amount summary.
- Bank Details and Amount in Words remain unchanged on the left side.
- GST calculations, grouping, totals, rounding and tax-mode behavior are unchanged.

### Unchanged

- Database schema: **10**
- Installer AppId: unchanged
- Quotation / Proforma / Tax Invoice business rules and calculations: unchanged
- Numbering and lifecycle rules: unchanged
- Existing v1.1.1 data remains compatible without migration.
- Mandatory pre-update backup, installer SHA-256/size verification, health validation and rollback protections remain enabled.
