# Quotation Manager v1.1.0

## Stage 2 completion and OTA candidate

v1.1.0 completes the first-class Proforma separation work and incorporates the R1 testing refinements while preserving the established Quotation and Tax Invoice workflows.

### Proforma Invoice

- Keeps Proforma Invoice as a fully separate first-class domain with its own workspace, history, repository/service/controller, numbering, templates, defaults, revisions, archive/restore and recovery behavior.
- Final Proforma exports are now **single-copy documents** only.
- Removes Customer Copy / Office Copy wording from final Proforma PDF, Word and Excel exports.
- Tax Invoice keeps its existing Customer/Office two-copy behavior unchanged.

### Quotation

- Adds side-effect-free **Preview** for workflow consistency with Proforma Invoice and Tax Invoice.
- Preview does not finalize the Quotation or consume/change its lifecycle state.
- Widens the Description column consistently across Quotation PDF, Word and Excel exports for better long-description readability.

### History

- Adds stable sorting across Quotation History, Proforma History and Tax Invoice History.
- Sortable fields include ID number, document number, date, customer, status, total amount and last modified.
- Supports ascending and descending order.
- Uses **ID number descending** as the stable default so autosave/updated timestamps no longer make records appear to jump around.
- Fixes Draft deletion eligibility, including Proforma's independent document-number field, while keeping non-Draft lifecycle records protected.

### Architecture and data safety

- Keeps Quotation, Proforma Invoice and Tax Invoice as separate concrete domains.
- Keeps shared financial/history infrastructure domain-neutral rather than importing concrete implementations across domains.
- Preserves clean-code guards preventing obsolete combined Quotation/Proforma runtime paths and removed discriminator/derived-link fields from returning.
- Database schema remains **10** for v1.0.13 Stage 2 R1 users.
- Existing schema-8 production installations continue to migrate transactionally through the validated schema 9 to 10 migrations.
- Existing PI numbers, lifecycle data, revisions and application data remain preserved.
- Installer AppId is unchanged for an in-place OTA upgrade.
